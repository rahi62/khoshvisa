# KhoshVisa — Competitor Research and Information Gain — 2026-10-17

## Research scope and evidence limitations
Focus: 4 new informational clusters for Persian-speaking visa applicants in Iran, distinct from existing 8 destination Money Pages and prior 9 guides.

For each topic, **10 relevant SERP-discovered source URLs** were collected and their accessible search-indexed content evaluated; ranking API was unnecessary. Direct page **open/read was attempted** for several competitor sources, with successful full-body access to some and failed fetch for others. Status is recorded **per URL**. **Do not describe the review as 40 fully read live competitor pages.** Search snippets, cached indexed text and full-body reads are distinctly classified. Official primary immigration laws, administrative guidance and VAC instructions verified separately. Google rank is not claimed.

**Interpretation:** Use competitor pages to discover question coverage and gaps; synthesize independently in Persian and verify all legal requirements using primary sources. No copying passages, promotional claims, invented success rates, or undocumented "official partnership."
