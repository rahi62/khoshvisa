# 2026-10-18 — Australia Visitor 600 targeted fact and content review

**Source of truth:** Home Affairs [Tourist stream — outside Australia](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/visitor-600/tourist-stream-overseas); do not accidentally rely on *inside Australia* page, despite the similar title.

## Verified distinctive content
- Submission and decision must occur while applicant is outside Australia; no work; generally tourist or family visit, up to 12 months dependent on grant.
- Australian immigration page explicitly says non-English documents need English translation **with originals**. Translations completed outside Australia need translator's name, address, phone and qualifications/experience; NAATI accreditation is mandatory for translators *in* Australia, not necessarily outside.
- Per-person applications; possible extra biometric / medical charges; prices must be date-checked. Embassy Iran tourist page https://iran.embassy.gov.au/tran/visitors.html exists but carries some potentially old operational descriptions and static '1 month' estimate; rely on current Home Affairs for processing time.
- March 25 2026 ministerial announcement temporarily restricted travel by certain Iranian passport-holder subclass 600 visa holders outside Australia for six months from March 26, 2026. Because period was six months, do NOT imply it is in force today (October 18) without fresh renewal evidence; no broad unsourced claims about current restrictions. Original: https://minister.homeaffairs.gov.au/TonyBurke/Pages/protecting-integrity-and-sustainability-of-our-migration-system.aspx
