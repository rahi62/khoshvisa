### Content gap and implementation
- Convert common GCKey vs Portal confusion into a practical account-selection explanation.
- Provide family processing cap rather than overgeneralizing per-person price; emphasize eligibility.
- Differentiate *initial application* optional upload slots from *post-submission, specifically requested* documents. Latest IRCC published instructions clarify account-dependent post-submission path and web-form fallback.
- Explain Application status tracker uses UCI and application number, not the portal invitation code.
- Keep Iranian applicability but do not promise particular VAC appointment availability, processing times or successful outcome.
