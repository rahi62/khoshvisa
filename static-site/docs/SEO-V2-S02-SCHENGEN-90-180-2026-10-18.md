## Aggregate content gap
1. Nine-plus competitors offer a **working calculator**, not merely a description. KhoshVisa currently links to the official EU calculator; we should not claim better interactivity without implementing and testing our own.
2. Most competitors use *visa-free* examples. Iran-resident holders of a Schengen type C visa also need to check visa-label **Duration of Stay**, validity and **Number of Entries** separately. KhoshVisa already covers this, and it remains a clear differentiator.
3. Most useful *information gain*: reproducible **precise calendar example** separating remaining days at entry from possible continuous length as old visits leave the rolling window.
4. Technical freshness risk: VizaHunt writes `27 countries`, inconsistent with current 29 Schengen participants. Do not echo third-party membership counts.
5. Official control: https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/border-crossing/short-stay-calculator_en and user manual. The Commission notes its calculator is advisory; competent border authorities decide entry/stay.

## Applied change
Enhanced example subsection: visits 2026-05-01 to 2026-05-30 (30 days), 2026-07-10 to 2026-08-08 (30 days), check/enter 2026-10-05 (60 consumed, 30 left), potential consecutive stay until 2026-12-03 (60 days) under the general 90/180 mathematical rule. Caveat: does **not** override the Iranian applicant's visa sticker limitations. Scenario cross-checked with acalculator's worked example. Code commit `6275fd8467789d844428b8c037229ed53853599a`.
